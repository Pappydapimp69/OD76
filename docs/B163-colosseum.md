# B163 Colosseum (spec, not built)

## Problem

Ranch training only pays off as a stronger start in wave runs. The player never sees the stats Pip trained (Heart Sense, Swift Pip, Star Power, Guardian Glow) win a fight on their own. Monster Rancher's core loop is train → tournament → rank up, and OD76 has the training without the tournament.

## Premise check (ZaneGPT)

- **"Ascended Pips" (plural) don't exist.** There's one Pip, and Pip Ascendant is a skill: 70% HEAT ignition, automatic drain (B94, B133). B163 has to invent the opponents.
  - Chosen: **rival Pips** generated by the game, one roster per rank. Cheapest; no backend.
  - Rejected for now: ghost Pips from old saves (needs a save-snapshot format) and live PvP (needs a server).
- **Ascended must stay a burst.** If a Colosseum Pip were permanently Ascended, B94/B131/B133 HEAT tuning would stop meaning anything. Both Pips ascend at 70% HEAT like today. The fight is about *when* each one does.
- **Fatigue, food and hygiene stay real.** A Colosseum fight is a battle test: same entry gate, same penalties.

## Change

### Entry
- The Arena gate gets a second choice: **Battle test** (unchanged) or **Colosseum**.
- Same entry rule as battle tests: refused at the B141 tired line, with the fatigue reading shown.
- One bracket per visit. A bracket costs a fixed fatigue amount (proposal: 15% of `fatigueMaxB118()`), charged once at entry.

### Opponents
- Each rank E–S has a roster of 8 named rival Pips (handler name, colour, temperament line).
- Each rival's stats are a **vector** over the four ranch stats, not one power number. Rank sets the total budget; each rival spends it differently (glass-cannon Star Power, tanky Guardian, fast Swift, Heart Sense scavenger).
- Rival budgets are built from the player's own Pip at entry: rank E = 80% of Pip's total, D 90%, C 100%, B 110%, A 120%, S 135%. That keeps the bracket near Pip's level without a fixed curve that drifts easy (the B108 lesson).
- Rival Pips own the Pip Ascendant skill at their rank's ascension level (E none, S asc-5).

### The fight
- Closed round ring, the rank's arena look (B125/B131). No waves and no hearts on the floor.
- Pip fights on his own using his trained stats. The player coaches instead of shooting:
  - **A** = Focus: Pip's next 3 shots home on the rival.
  - **X** = Ascend: fires Pip Ascendant if HEAT ≥ 70%. Otherwise a short "not yet" buzz.
  - **B** = Guard: Guardian Glow shield up, Pip doesn't attack while it's held.
- HEAT fills from hits landed and hits blocked, so passive play still builds toward Ascend.
- Rival Pips use the same three commands on a simple AI (temperament weights).
- **Win:** rival's HP to 0, or more HP left when the 60-second clock ends. A draw goes to whoever landed more hits.
- HP scales with stat budget and damage stays below it (Brain duel lesson): fights last 20–40 s at even stats instead of one-shots. No per-hit percent cap (it erased burst wins in testing).

### Bracket
- Single elimination, 8 entrants: Pip plus 7 rivals from the rank roster.
- **Fold seeding** (Brain): seeds by stat total, round 1 = 1v8, 4v5, 2v7, 3v6, so the top two can only meet in the final.
- Between rounds: one free snack choice (B118 snack list) or a full HP heal, not both.
- Losing ends the bracket. No heart or stone loss; Pip goes home tired.

### Rewards and ranks
- Prize by rounds won: Heart Stones 1/2/4 (QF/SF/final win), times the B108 rank multiplier (×1.0–2.0).
- Winning the bracket at your highest unlocked rank unlocks the next rank. It counts the same as B108's stage-5 clear: either path unlocks.
- A trophy shelf at Pip's bed shows each rank's bracket win (cosmetic).

### Hype layer (Brain)
- One-line announcer cards at fight start, at Ascend, at under 25% HP and at the knockout, plus a crowd-noise swell on the shared sound engine (`b21-81.js`).
- Announcer and crowd use a **cosmetic** RNG stream, separate from the fight stream (Brain: cosmetic rolls on the shared stream changed sim results).

### Determinism
- The fight sim uses its own seeded PRNG stream (`colosseumRngB163`), seeded per bracket.
- Fight outcomes are tested by seed: same seed + same command timeline = same winner.

## Acceptance

- The gate offers Colosseum and refuses at the tired line exactly like battle tests.
- Fold seeding pairs 1v8, 4v5, 2v7, 3v6, and seeds 1 and 2 can only meet in the final.
- Rival budgets match the rank percentages against Pip's entry stats.
- Ascend is refused under 70% HEAT for both Pips.
- Same seed + command timeline gives the same result; cosmetic rolls don't change it.
- An even-stat fight lasts 20–40 s over 50 seeds; no first-hit knockouts.
- Prizes, rank multiplier and the rank unlock are exact; a loss costs no hearts or stones.

## Open fork (Brain tension, 🔴 open)

> Does a multi-axis power vector let a lower-tier character win legibly, or does the tier ceiling still decide almost every fight?

Brain's toy roster had the higher tier win 25 of 28 duels even with vector stats. Two consequences for B163:
- Don't show a single "power" number for rivals unless it's monotone in the real stats (0 inversions). Show the four stat bars instead.
- Playtest question: at even budget, does a matchup counter (Guardian vs Star Power, say) win often enough to feel like strategy? If not, the vector is cosmetic and coaching has to carry it.
