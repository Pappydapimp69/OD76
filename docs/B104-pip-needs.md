# B104 — Pip's needs

## Problem

Fatigue was Pip's only need. The ranch should ask you to look after him.

## Change

- **Hunger** and **hygiene** (0–100, start 80) join fatigue on the ranch HUD. The HUD uses icons on phones, and the Bag has its own button.
- **Ranch week:** every drill, rest and battle test is a ranch week (battle tests now advance the week too). Each week: hunger −12, hygiene −6 (rest), −14 (drill) or −20 (battle test).
- **Food stall:** Pip Pellets (♥ 15, food +25), Berry Bun (♥ 30, food +40, fatigue −5), Bubble Soap (♥ 10).
- **Wash tub:** one soap, hygiene +50.
- **Pip's menu:** press A at Pip to pet him or feed him anything in the bag.
- **Effects:**
  - Below 40 hunger: solo success −15% and drills add +10 fatigue.
  - Below 40 hygiene: solo success −10%.
  - Below 20 hunger: Swift and Star Power −1 for the next battle test.
  - Below 20 hygiene: Heart Sense and Guardian Glow −1 for the next battle test.
- Pip droops (🍽) when hungry and shows smudges when grubby.

## Verified

Covered by the B105 full-bundle run.
